# Renewable Energy DAO

A decentralized autonomous organization for investment and governance of renewable energy assets on the Stacks blockchain.

## Overview

Renewable Energy DAO enables participants to invest in renewable energy projects through fractional ownership, distribute revenue, and participate in governance decisions using a transparent, on-chain voting system. This platform bridges the gap between renewable energy infrastructure and decentralized finance, allowing for democratic access to green energy investments.

## Features

- **Fractional Asset Ownership**: Purchase shares in renewable energy assets like solar farms, wind turbines, and hydroelectric plants
- **Revenue Distribution**: Receive proportional revenue from energy generation based on share ownership
- **Governance Participation**: Propose and vote on key decisions affecting asset management
- **Performance Tracking**: Monitor energy output, operational efficiency, and environmental impact
- **Transparent Operations**: All transactions and governance actions are recorded on the Stacks blockchain

## Smart Contract Functions

### Asset Management

- `register-renewable-asset`: Creates a new renewable energy asset with specified parameters
- `purchase-asset-shares`: Allows users to purchase available shares in an energy asset
- `get-asset-details`: Retrieves comprehensive information about a specific asset

### Revenue Distribution

- `distribute-asset-revenue`: Distributes revenue generated by an asset to all shareholders
- `claim-revenue`: Allows shareholders to claim their proportional revenue

### Governance

- `create-governance-proposal`: Enables shareholders to propose operational changes
- `vote-on-proposal`: Allows shareholders to cast votes on active proposals
- `get-proposal-details`: Retrieves details about a specific governance proposal

## Asset Types

The platform currently supports the following renewable energy types:
- Solar
- Wind
- Hydro
- Biomass

## Technical Specifications

- **Blockchain**: Stacks
- **Smart Contract Language**: Clarity
- **Minimum Investment**: 1 STX
- **Share Precision**: 6 decimal places
- **Proposal Approval Threshold**: 75%
- **Maintenance Window**: ~24 hours (144 blocks)

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- A Stacks wallet (Hiro Wallet recommended)
- STX tokens for investment and transaction fees

### Investing in an Asset

1. Review available assets using `get-asset-details`
2. Choose an asset with "active" operational status
3. Purchase shares using `purchase-asset-shares` function
4. Track your investment through `get-shareholder-details`

### Proposing Governance Changes

1. Ensure you hold at least 5% voting power in the asset
2. Create a proposal with `create-governance-proposal`
3. Specify category, details, and any requested funds
4. Monitor voting progress through `get-proposal-details`

## Governance Rules

- **Minimum Quorum**: 50% of total voting power required for valid decisions
- **Voting Period**: ~24 hours (144 blocks)
- **Approval Threshold**: 75% minimum approval for proposals to pass
- **Execution Cooldown**: ~12 hours (72 blocks) delay before implementation

## Security Considerations

The contract implements various security measures:
- Unauthorized access prevention
- Self-transfer prohibition
- Zero-quantity transaction prevention
- Invalid asset status checks
- Quorum requirements for governance actions
